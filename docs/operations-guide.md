# FLEET OPERATIONS & DOCKMASTER MANUAL

## 1. Dockmaster & Slip Operations (`/admin/marina`)
- Oversee live marina map, slip occupancy rates, daily check-in arrivals, and check-out clearances.
- Log vessel arrival condition ratings and utility meter usage (Power, Water, Blackwater pump-out).

---

## 2. Vessel Readiness & STCW Compliance (`/admin/operations`)
- Validate 4-step vessel readiness checklist before approving charter departure.
- Verify STCW crew certifications, captain licenses, and blocking maintenance counters.
