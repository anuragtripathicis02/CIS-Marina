# CUSTOMER PORTAL & LUXURY UX SPECIFICATION

## 1. Customer Web Portal (`/portal`)
Designed as a luxury hospitality experience prioritizing generous spacing, dark slate & gold accents, and simple navigation.

---

## 2. Customer Dashboard & Navigation
- **Header Navigation**: `Dashboard`, `My Bookings`, `Explore Fleet`, `Services`, `Bespoke Concierge`, `Yacht Club`, `Support`, `✨ AI Concierge`.
- **Smartphone Bottom Navigation Bar**: Fixed bottom bar for mobile screen sizes (`Home`, `Bookings`, `Concierge`, `AI`, `Profile`).
- **Welcome Banner**: Displays active membership tier (`Gold Flagship Member`) and upcoming charter experience card.
