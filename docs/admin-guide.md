# ADMIN & OPERATOR USER MANUAL

## 1. Executive BI & Dashboard (`/admin/executive`)
- Access high-level KPIs, 6-month revenue forecasts, demand heatmaps, fleet performance indicators, and predictive maintenance risk alerts.
- Use the **Ask AI Analytics** assistant to ask natural language questions with permission-scoped tools.

---

## 2. Enterprise Branch & Currency Management (`/admin/enterprise`)
- Configure multi-branch hierarchy (`Organization` → `Country` → `Branch`).
- Manage regional tax rates (`TaxRule`) and historical exchange rate snapshots (`ExchangeRateProvider`).
- Review immutable security audit logs and process GDPR privacy requests (`ACCESS`, `EXPORT`, `DELETION`).

---

## 3. Operations & Marina Management (`/admin/operations` & `/admin/marina`)
- Monitor vessel operational readiness, crew STCW certs, pre-charter inspection checklists, and maintenance tickets.
- Manage marina docks, berths, slip contracts, check-ins, check-outs, and utility bunkering services.
